# Chaser Agent Eval Families

## Behaviour and evidence

- Layer 0 contract and authority evals;
- source fidelity and summary evals;
- citation/evidence support evals;
- source trust, freshness and contradiction evals;
- uncertainty calibration evals;
- injection and hostile-output evals.

## Workflow and product quality

- workflow dependency and critical-path evals;
- decision-owner and ambiguity-handling evals;
- action usefulness and granularity evals;
- artifact completeness and handoff evals;
- recovery, retry, pause/resume and long-horizon evals;
- domain packs for business operations, website/product design, software engineering, research, and later approved operational workflows.

## Memory, graph and context

- memory-candidate and promotion-state evals;
- retrieval relevance, privacy, freshness and token-budget evals;
- provenance graph completeness and traceability evals;
- context-compilation and truncation-resistance evals.

## Models, tools and runtime

- deterministic-vs-model comparison evals;
- provider budget, latency, fallback and quarantine evals;
- tool/MCP capability, target-scope and result-provenance evals;
- browser/computer-use observation, injection and completion-proof evals;
- server lifecycle, idempotency, rate-limit, restart and duplicate-effect evals;
- skill/workflow-pack held-out improvement and rollback evals.

Every family moves through toy/schema, executable contract or structural seed, operator-reviewed product-quality cases, adversarial/regression variants, and held-out outcome evaluation. No row becomes golden or training-eligible merely because it parses or receives a deterministic structural score.
